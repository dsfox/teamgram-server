# Reaction pictures

The animations and icons in this directory are Google's animated Noto emoji
(Noto Emoji Animation, https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changed for ice9 by tools/reaction_assets.py: each Lottie animation is
compressed with gzip into a TGS file, with "tgs": 1 added at its root, and
each icon is the first frame of the 512-pixel WebP, saved as a still image.
